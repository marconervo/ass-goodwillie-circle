An Adams SS for the Goodwillie tower of the circle

This tool computes an Adams \(E_2\)-page for studying the \(p\)-local homotopy groups of \(P_{p^k}I(S^1)\), for a fixed prime \(p\) and approximation degree \(k\).

Mathematical explanation

The construction reduces this unstable problem to a calculation with spectra.

By [Ner26, Theorem C], there is a \(p\)-local equivalence \[ \Omega P_{p^k}I(S^1) \simeq \mathbb{Z}\times\Omega^{\infty+2k}\tau_{>0}\mathbb{SP}^{p^k} \] where \(\mathbb{SP}^{m}\) is the \(m\)th symmetric product spectrum and \(\tau_{>0}\) denotes its connective cover. Thus the spectrum \[ \mathbb{Z}\oplus\Sigma^{-2k}\tau_{>0}\mathbb{SP}^{p^k} \] provides a stable model for the loop space, and its Adams spectral sequence gives access to the homotopy groups of the Goodwillie approximation, with a shift of one degree.

Nakaoka's calculation [Nak58, §5, Main Theorem] identifies \[ H^*(\mathbb{SP}^{p^k};\mathbb{F}_p) \cong \mathcal{A}\big/\bigl(\mathcal{A}\beta+\mathcal{A}_{>k}\bigr) \] Here \(\mathcal{A}\) is the mod-\(p\) Steenrod algebra, \(\beta\) is the Bockstein, and \(\mathcal{A}_{>k}\) is the length-filtration left ideal spanned by admissible monomials of length at least \(k+1\). Equivalently, a basis of the quotient consists of all admissible monomials of length at most \(k\) that do not end in the Bockstein.

The calculator uses the resolution software ext-rs of Chatham, Chua, and Beauvais-Feisthauer [CCB] to compute \[ E_2^{s,t}=\operatorname{Ext}_{\mathcal{A}}^{s,t} \bigl(H^*(\mathbb{SP}^{p^k};\mathbb{F}_p),\mathbb{F}_p\bigr) \] Only the part of Nakaoka's presentation needed in the requested degree range is supplied to the resolver. The chart displays this stable \(E_2\)-page in homotopy degree \(q=t-s-2k+1\) for \(q\ge2\), together with the vertical tower at \(q=1\) for the separate \(\mathbb{Z}\)-factor.

Differentials and hidden extensions are not computed.
